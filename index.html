Full package in detail

What “full options” actually covers

Recruitment is the core. Everything else is designed so the specialist and their family can focus on the new role and life, not on bureaucracy and logistics.

1

Talent search & placement

We define the role with you, map the market, screen candidates, manage interviews and support the offer stage until acceptance.

2

Employment & HR setup

Contracts compliant with local labour law, registration with social security, payroll setup and ongoing HR coordination if required.

3

Residence & work authorisation

Guidance and support through the correct residence and work permit route for the employee and eligible family members.

4

Tax, bank & formal registrations

Tax identification number, bank account opening, social security number and other mandatory registrations in the new country.

5

Housing & rental

Short-term accommodation on arrival plus search and negotiation of a longer-term rental that fits the family and budget.

6

Documents & recognition

Driving licence exchange, diploma and professional qualification recognition, apostilles and certified translations where needed.

7

Language & integration

Language training options for the employee and family members, plus practical orientation for daily life in the new country.

8

Schools & family support

Research and shortlisting of schools, application support and guidance so children can settle into education with minimal disruption.
